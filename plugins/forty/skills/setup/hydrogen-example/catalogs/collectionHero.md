# collectionHero

A full-width banner for one collection: its image with a large headline, an optional supporting line and call to action over it, linking to the collection's page.

## When to Use

One collection is clearly the answer and deserves the spotlight — the visitor named a category ("I'm after loungewear"), or a turn should open with a strong lead before the products that follow.

## Don't Use When

Several collections are in play — use `collectionList`. The visitor wants to pick items now — use `productGrid`. Never more than one per turn.
