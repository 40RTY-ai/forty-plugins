# productDetail

One product, large, with a one-sentence reason it is the right pick — for when the visitor wants a single answer ("which one should I get") or has chosen one and wants to buy it. Shows its image, name, price, option choice and add-to-cart.

## When to Use

The visitor wants a single answer — "which one should I get", "the best one for everyday", "tell me about the seamless brief" — or they picked one from a grid or comparison and want to buy it.

## Don't Use When

Several products fit equally — use `productGrid`. They are weighing a few — use `comparisonTable`.
